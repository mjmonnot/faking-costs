The paper’s primary meta-analytic estimates are per trait (Table 1 and Supplemental Table S2): each Big Five factor keeps its own sample-size-weighted mean correlation with GMA. The aggregation below is an additional descriptive view — it pools the five trait-level estimates within each stakes group with inverse-variance weights w = N / (1 − r²)² and ranks the groups. It does not replace the trait-specific estimates used in the simulations and calculator.
